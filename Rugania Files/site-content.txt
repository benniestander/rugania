2. ABOUT US
Headline: A Legacy of Conservation and Wild Beauty.

Body Copy:
Rugania’s story is written in the unique topography of our "floating hills" and the life-giving waters of the Selons River. Our 4,000-hectare estate is a malaria-free haven where evergreen landscapes meet rugged gorges, creating a sanctuary for a staggering variety of South African game.

Our Philosophy: Sustainable Majesty
At Rugania, we don’t just host nature; we honor it. Our park operates on the principles of sustainable utilization. Through careful population balance and responsible, eco-friendly hunting practices, we ensure that the Rugania you see today will be even more vibrant for the generations to come.

Why Rugania?

Malaria-Free: Safe for the whole family.

Diverse Ecosystems: From riverine forests to hilltop vistas.

Commitment to Nature: Every visit supports our conservation mission.

3. LEISURE & ACTIVITIES
Headline: Adventure at Your Own Pace.

Body Copy:
Not every adventure requires a rifle. At Rugania, the bush offers a thousand ways to reconnect.

Sunrise & Sunset Game Drives: Board our custom 4x4 Land Cruiser or the rugged Unimog. From the towering Giraffe to the elusive Leopard and Brown Hyena, witness Africa’s "hidden" residents in their natural habitat.

Bird Watching: A paradise for enthusiasts. Quiet your mind at our waterholes and watch the colors of the sky descend to drink.

Mountain Bike Trails: Bring your bike and test your mettle on trails ranging from "Bushveld Breeze" easy to "Gorge Challenge" difficult.

Guided Nature Walks: Follow in the footsteps of our expert guides to discover the small wonders—the medicinal plants, the tracks in the sand, and the stories of the soil.

4. HUNTING
Headline: The Ultimate Challenge. The Perfect Shot.

Body Copy:
Rugania is a premier destination for both the seasoned rifleman and the dedicated bowhunter. Our unique topography—combining vast open plains with dense, strategic cover—provides a fair-chase environment that tests your skill and rewards your patience.

Rifle Hunting: Dedicated areas designed for safety and optimal sightlines.
Bow Hunting: We specialize in the art of the bow, featuring expertly placed hides and blinds that put you at the heart of the action.

Why Hunt With Us?

Exemption Status: No hunter’s license is required.

All-Season Access: Prime hunting from April to August, with year-round availability.

Full Support: We provide necessary transport permits for meat and biltong.

Non-Hunter Friendly: Bring the family; while you hunt, they can enjoy game drives and the luxury of our lodges.

5. ACCOMMODATION
Headline: Your Home in the Heart of the Bush.

Body Copy:
After a day under the African sun, retreat to comfort. Rugania offers four distinct ways to stay, ranging from hilltop luxury to the raw magic of a bush camp.

Aalwynpark Visitors Lodge | Luxury with a View
Perched on a hill with a 360-degree view of the bushveld, this 6-bedroom lodge is the crown jewel of Rugania. Features a splash pool, pool table, and a massive boma for nights under the stars. (Sleeps 16)

Naauwkloof Hunters Lodge | The Authentic Farmhouse
The quintessential hunter’s retreat. A classic farmhouse on a rise, offering 4 bedrooms and 3 bathrooms. Perfect for groups who want a cozy, communal atmosphere. (Sleeps 14)

The Picturesque Farmhouse | Timeless Elegance
Step back in time. With original wooden floors and vintage charm, this restored farmhouse is for those who appreciate the grace of a bygone era.

The Lapa | The Real Bush Camp
Unplug and unwind. For the purist who wants the sound of the night wind and the glow of the fire—without the city distractions.
